# Aggregate acceptance

Read after each integration affecting evidence and before overall acceptance/reporting. Resolve Work On's `references/verification.md` and `references/completion.md` from the compatible package recorded at entry, and read them completely as procedure libraries. Reading these files does not invoke single-change delivery. Work On owns the common snapshot/check/finding/repair/gate procedures; Work On Spec owns their whole-agreement inputs and additional cross-ticket obligations below. Do not copy those procedures or interpret child gate passes as aggregate evidence.

## 1. Establish overall coverage and the integrated snapshot

Inspect actual target files/diff and every consumed ticket return, raw results and local decisions. Map all spec acceptance IDs to delivered tickets and integrated behavior, including obligations spanning several tickets, shared interfaces, sequencing, failure/recovery paths and deferred/excluded commitments. Unassigned or missing scope remains a gap; do not create a parent implementation to cover it silently. Only the agreement owner can approve changed commitments.

Supply the verification library with the full current agreement/revision, protected/excluded work, integrated scope and supporting inputs, baseline, criterion/risk mapping and cross-ticket check strategy. Use discovered Review Delivery's maintained full committed/dirty snapshot contract. Freeze scoped writes/integration during checks/review. Inspect equivalent local evidence before reuse; rerun state/integration-sensitive checks. Changed deciding inputs invalidate dependent results; uncertain impact invalidates the relevant set, preserving history.

**Done:** every spec obligation and material cross-ticket risk has an inspected integrated disposition and current evidence or named gap. The common verification procedure's final path/fingerprint comparison succeeds or drift is recorded as a blocker.

## 2. Obtain aggregate review and disposition findings

Discover compatible `review-delivery`, read its complete entry and relevant references, and supply the same pinned overall agreement/scope and raw evidence to independent Standards and Spec contexts. The root reserves/launches them under the resolved conditional coordination library and existing root assignment record; same-session evidence may remain in conversation. Ticket authors cannot review their own contributions as independent reviewers. Axes may run sequentially with one available slot; unfinished ticket agents still consume capacity. If capacity cannot safely be released or independence is unavailable, retain implemented-but-unaccepted progress.

Consume the completion library for finding disposition and correction, giving it a stable final-integration checkpoint and the existing root ledger. Keep each finding's lineage and ticket counts. Genuinely new cross-ticket failures can belong to the integration checkpoint; relocating an unresolved local cause cannot reset its budget. Assign corrections to the behavior owner through bounded Work On scope or perform a specifically authorized lead correction with reserved ownership; this is not a direct replacement for ticket scheduling. Inspect returns and integrate serially through dispatch. Repin and refresh affected checks and significant-change review. Unknown history, missing capability, unsafe authority, disagreement or exhaustion blocks affected correction under the resolved coordination/completion libraries.

**Done:** current independent axis reports and all material findings have inspected dispositions, or precise blockers preserve artifacts, original reports and spent history.

## 3. Decide overall gates and report remaining work

Apply the completion library's separate build/accept/report gates to the overall agreement, not a sum of child successes. Include documentation/changelog disposition and affected tracker readiness under exact authority. Recompute scheduling eligibility through dispatch using native evidence; readiness assessment alone neither rewires a graph nor unlocks a prerequisite requiring accepted merge.

Return per-ticket and aggregate evidence pointers, integrated/not-integrated state, current/unrun/stale checks/review, approved deferrals and remaining required scope, finding/checkpoint lineage, model reservation/usage history and next actions. Return delivery status without discovering `pr` for an unrequested next stage. Only a user-selected PR stage uses that companion with current evidence; an absent unrequested PR capability or hypothetical future-session storage destination is not a delivery blocker. Retain actual acceptance/merge/issue state. Reporting a blocker can complete honestly while acceptance remains blocked. Merging, closing, installing and releasing are separate actions.

**Aggregate completion:** every whole-agreement obligation is accounted for in current verified, unverified or blocked scope with distinct gates and an evidence-backed next action; child passes alone never establish overall acceptance.
