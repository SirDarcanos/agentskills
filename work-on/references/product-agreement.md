# Product agreement adapter

Read completely when entry selects unresolved product intent, spec expression/publication or changed approved commitments. Use the inspected context and [handoff contract](contracts.md#handoff-envelope). This is an internal procedure of an explicitly requested Work On run. For changes or validation, read [adapter validation](product-agreement-validation.md).

## 1. Inspect the agreement and select the gap

Inspect the request, existing authoritative brief/spec and approval evidence, current deciding inputs, protected work and allowed effects. Reuse a current actionable agreement without discovery or rewriting. A small clear supplied request may remain a proportionate brief in the lead record; it needs no spec issue or hierarchy. Missing or changed commitments require their owner's decision, not a technical guess.

**Done:** the existing agreement is usable, or the dependent discovery/spec responsibility is identified with scope, authority and preserved decisions.

## 2. Consume the required specialist return

For unresolved intent, discover compatible `shape-work` using [capability checks](contracts.md#capability-checks). Read its complete entry/relevant references and record source/revision or digest. Supply the shared envelope and inspect its actual cited brief, approval status, four concern dispositions, gaps and deciding-input freshness. It alone owns discovery rounds and fact-finding. Missing/incompatible/ambiguous capability blocks needed discovery, not reuse of a current agreement.

For specification expression, revision or authorized publication, discover compatible `write-spec` the same way. Supply the agreement/discovery brief and current evidence, existing acceptance IDs, home/approval owner, protected scope, exact content/write/publication/readiness authority and expected return. It alone owns spec fidelity, approval, authoritative reconciliation and optional publication/read-back. Inspect the actual artifact for observable acceptance, preserved decisions/dispositions, draft versus approved commitments and separate publication/readiness results. No Shape Work session is required for ordinary agreed input; current complete evidence needs no new specialist run.

Execute specialists in the lead's context unless delegation is separately authorized. Helper names or success labels are not proof; missing capabilities block only dependent routes. No implicit installation or legacy command substitution.

**Done:** required specialist outputs are inspected/current, or useful draft progress and specific blockers are retained.

## 3. Return to delivery routing

Return through the shared envelope: agreement artifact/revision, acceptance IDs, approval evidence, concern dispositions, source freshness, actual document/publication outcomes, independent readiness rationale and limitations, protected work and next action. Missing approval or required authoritative reconciliation blocks reliance on affected commitments.

The lead chooses the next unmet responsibility, normally technical readiness for an actionable agreement; reuse current readiness instead of rerunning it. Standalone specialists stop at their own returns. Product completion is not build readiness, accepted implementation, issue closure or release.
