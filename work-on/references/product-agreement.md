# Product agreement

Read completely when entry selects unresolved product intent or an approved product commitment changes. Use the inspected context and [handoff contract](contracts.md#handoff-envelope); this is an internal procedure of an explicitly requested Work On run, not a separate command. For changes or validation, read [product-agreement validation](product-agreement-validation.md).

## 1. Reuse the agreement and choose its size

Inspect the request, existing brief/spec revision, relevant product documents and accepted decisions. Record the authoritative product home and approval owner from target policy. Compare intent with actual evidence; keep unchanged commitments and reopen only missing, contradictory or changed branches. If no product home is established, recommend a proportionate location and obtain agreement before making it authoritative.

Choose a lightweight brief for a small clear request whose relevant behavior, consequences and acceptance are already understood. A brief may remain in the conversation or approved task artifact under target retention policy; a multi-session handoff follows the contract's durable-record requirement. Use a fuller spec when unresolved workflows or consequential choices need discovery, or when target policy requires it. A document spec need not become an issue; publication is a separate authorized action.

**Complete when:** the current agreement, missing decisions, product home, approval owner and brief/spec route are identified. An existing approved agreement with no relevant gaps can proceed directly to fidelity/readiness assessment.

## 2. Consume discovery from Shape Work

If step 1 finds unresolved product intent, discover compatible `shape-work` through the runtime/project catalog or an explicit approved location using [capability checks](contracts.md#capability-checks). Read its complete entry and relevant references and record resolved source/revision or digest. Shape Work is the sole owner of discovery rounds, fact-finding routes and concern dispositions; this adapter carries no duplicate discovery procedure. Missing/incompatible/ambiguous capability blocks the dependent discovery, not reuse of an already actionable agreement. Do not substitute an installed grilling command or install a helper implicitly.

Supply the shared envelope: target, outcome and agreement/acceptance references, current findings/deciding identities, confirmed decisions, unresolved intent, product home/approval owner, protected work, exact authority, finite bound and expected discovery brief. Execute in the lead's context unless separate delegation authority exists. Inspect returned facts and their cited support, observable goal/behavior/success, approval versus draft status, preserved concern dispositions, source freshness, actual document outcomes and specific blockers. A companion's completion label alone is not agreement evidence.

Reuse a current complete discovery brief without invoking Shape Work again. Changed deciding inputs reopen only dependent questions. With an actionable confirmed brief, the active authorized Work On run continues to step 3; standalone Shape Work ends at its return. An unresolved consequential decision or required unreconciled product record blocks reliance on the affected agreement; retain useful draft findings and the owner's next action. The lead retains delivery authority and routine engineering choice.

**Complete when:** current discovery is inspected and usable, or dependent discovery has an explicit blocker with preserved findings. Spec writing and publication remain this procedure's responsibilities until their separate extraction.

## 3. Express and confirm observable agreement

Write only the detail this request needs, whether brief or full spec:

1. Intended outcome, affected users/workflows and rationale.
2. Observable behavior, including material exceptions and recovery; give stable acceptance IDs with checkable outcomes. Avoid criteria that only assert an implementation technique or a vague quality claim.
3. Required scope, exclusions, deferred concerns and accepted limitations, preserving their rationale and revisit conditions.
4. Confirmed decisions and approval source; clearly marked proposals, assumptions and remaining questions. Link reused decisions rather than restating unrelated policy.
5. Relevant authority/dependency pointers and the next responsibility. Product approval alone does not establish technical/build readiness or grant publishing authority.

Check fidelity against the request and existing agreements: no silent new commitments, invented approval or discarded deferred concern. Present changed consequential commitments and their practical effects to the approval owner through the target's collaboration style. Until approved, mark the artifact draft and block only actions needing that decision.

When approved commitments change, update the **existing authoritative product document** within approved write authority, including acceptance and rationale. Preserve prior decisions/history and link any supersession; a new issue or summary must point to that home rather than becoming a duplicate policy source. If required document writes are unapproved or unavailable, report the proposed delta and block reliance on an unreconciled agreement. Meaning/model or qualifying ADR changes use the compatible domain-modeling handoff from [contracts](contracts.md#capability-checks).

**Complete when:** behavior and material acceptance are observable, concern dispositions survive, consequential changes have explicit approval, and the authoritative product record reflects approved commitments. A draft return remains valid but is not an approved agreement.

## 4. Publish and assess readiness separately

Skip issue publication when the brief/document route suffices. If publication is requested, inspect target tracker/title/label policies and live mappings, verify read access, check existing issue identity to avoid duplicates, and confirm exact mutation authority. Use the compatible maintained tracker procedure from [contracts](contracts.md#capability-checks) for affected operations. Missing permissions or mappings block publication, not local discovery; propose a resolution rather than invent labels or change project vocabulary.

For an authorized spec issue:

1. Follow the target's approved title convention, whether plain descriptive titles or required prefixes. Classify by purpose and mapped roles, never by parsing the title.
2. Apply the mapped **specification role**, not the mapped **implementation-step role**. The roles are mutually exclusive. If an existing assignment conflicts, propose the exact correction and require authority before removing it. Assess other classifications independently and preserve unrelated labels and native relationships.
3. Assess readiness from evidence independently of publication: an approved agreement with resolved material shared design decisions can receive the project's mapped ready role under policy; a draft or unresolved material design cannot. Parent readiness permits coordination of eligible children, not duplicate implementation or bypassing native blockers. Unavailable technical evidence routes to technical readiness rather than being guessed here.
4. Apply only authorized readiness additions/removals; inspect stale readiness when approval is missing or decisions changed. If correction is unauthorized, report the contradictory assignment and block reliance on it. A readiness label is not approval.
5. Read back the persisted identity, title, body/agreement pointer and revision, mapped spec role, absence of the implementation-step role, independently assessed classifications and readiness. Compare with the intended delta. After timeout or partial failure, inspect actual state before retrying. Failed/mismatched read-back is unknown or partial publication, never success.

**Complete when:** either no publication is needed, or exact authorized publication and role/readiness assignments have matching read-back. Record publication and readiness separately; a published draft can be unready. Unmet shared design decisions block readiness, not authorized draft publication.

## 5. Return the agreement handoff

Return through [contracts](contracts.md#handoff-envelope): artifact/source revision, acceptance IDs, approval status/evidence, four concern dispositions, outstanding assumptions/proposals, authoritative document changes or justified no-change decision, publication/read-back outcome, readiness rationale and specific blockers. Retain uncertain operations and protected work.

The lead inspects the actual agreement for fidelity, observability, approval and preserved deferrals, then selects the next responsibility. An approved actionable product agreement normally goes to technical readiness; inspect its destination before loading it. If absent, report it unavailable with the handoff. Product completion is not technical readiness, accepted implementation, issue closure or release.
