# Product-agreement adapter validation

Read when changing or validating [product agreement](product-agreement.md). Follow [entry validation](validation.md#reproducible-static-checks) for package checks. Discovery behavior belongs to the discovered Shape Work validation reference; specification fidelity, approval and publication behavior belongs to discovered Write Spec validation. Resolve those packages normally, not through sibling paths.

Inspect entry/contracts/adapter together:

1. Current actionable agreement bypasses new discovery/writing; a small clear supplied request can remain a brief with no hierarchy.
2. Unresolved intent supplies the shared envelope to compatible Shape Work; inspect sources, approval, four dispositions and gaps.
3. Needed spec expression/revision/publication supplies inspected agreement and exact effects to compatible Write Spec; inspect actual acceptance, approval, authoritative reconciliation and separate publication/readiness read-back.
4. Missing/incompatible/ambiguous helpers block only their dependent route; no legacy commands or installation fallback.
5. Standalone specialists stop at their returns; the authorized Work On caller chooses the next unmet responsibility without treating product completion as build readiness.

These are expected checks until executed evidence is separately recorded. Keep #20 and #14's historical repository records at their existing paths; extraction does not retroactively execute them. No duplicate spec-writing/publication procedure remains in this adapter.
