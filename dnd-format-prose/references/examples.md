# Transformation examples

These examples are original calibration material. They demonstrate information handling and transformation depth; they are not templates to repeat mechanically.

## 1. Ordinary description to read-aloud prose

**Mode:** Voice-only

**Source**

> The chapel has six cracked stone benches. Rain comes through a hole in the roof. A bronze bell is lying beneath the altar, and it rings softly whenever lightning flashes.

**Output**

> Six cracked stone benches face the chapel's altar. Rain slips through a hole in the roof, darkening the floor around a fallen bronze bell. With every flash of lightning, the bell gives a low, solitary ring.

The rewrite changes order and rhythm but preserves every object, relationship, and event.

## 2. Direct address to neutral observation

**Request:** Remove direct address and assumptions about the party.

**Mode:** Voice-only

**Source**

> You enter the gallery and see portraits watching you from both walls. As you walk toward the far door, their painted eyes follow you.

**Output**

> Portraits line both walls of the gallery. Their painted eyes follow any movement toward the door at the far end.

The requested perspective change removes “you” while preserving the portraits' behavior. Without that request, inherited direct address could remain.

## 3. Mixed visible information, secret lore, and mechanics

**Mode:** Voice-only

**Source**

> A dry fountain stands in the courtyard. Its basin is full of black feathers. The feathers belong to the wereraven Mara, who watches the courtyard from the eastern tower. A character who searches the fountain and succeeds on a DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds her silver key beneath a loose tile.

**Output**

### Read Aloud

> A dry fountain stands at the center of the courtyard, its basin filled with black feathers.

### DM Lore

> The feathers belong to Mara, a wereraven watching the courtyard from the eastern tower. Her silver key is hidden beneath a loose tile in the fountain.

### Mechanics

> A character who searches the fountain and succeeds on a DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the key.

The split prevents the read-aloud passage from revealing Mara, the hidden key, or the search procedure.

## 4. Plain creature information to bestiary lore

**Mode:** Voice-only

**Source**

> Mire hounds live in flooded forests. They hunt alone at night. They cover uneaten prey with mud and return to it later. Frogs stop calling when a mire hound is nearby.

**Output**

> Mire hounds haunt flooded forests, hunting alone after nightfall. Each buries what it cannot eat beneath the mud and returns on later nights. The forest often gives the first warning of a hound's approach: frogs fall silent while it is near.

No anatomy, motive, supernatural origin, or additional behavior is invented.

## 5. Voice-only transformation of modern prose

**Mode:** Voice-only

**Source**

> The server stores customer records in three databases. An administrator reviews failed login attempts every morning and locks accounts after five failures.

**Output**

> The server keeps customer records across three databases. Each morning, an administrator reviews failed login attempts; five failures are enough to lock an account.

Voice-only mode keeps the modern referents. D&D cadence does not authorize replacing technical facts with fantasy objects.

## 6. Diegetic adaptation of modern prose

**Request:** Adapt the system as a fantasy archive while preserving its relationships.

**Mode:** Diegetic adaptation

**Source**

> The server stores customer records in three databases. An administrator reviews failed login attempts every morning and locks accounts after five failures.

**Output**

> The archive keeps each patron's record in three separate ledgers. Every morning, the chief scribe reviews failed attempts to open them; after five failures, the patron's seal is barred.

The adaptation changes server → archive, databases → ledgers, administrator → chief scribe, login attempts → attempts to open, accounts → patron seals. The count, schedule, storage relationship, and lockout consequence remain intact.

## 7. Overdecorated fantasy prose to restrained lore

**Mode:** Voice-only

**Source**

> Beneath the eternally weeping heavens, the impossibly ancient tower claws its way upward like the accusing finger of some long-dead and forgotten god, its stones whispering countless sorrowful secrets into the mournful wind.

**Output**

> Under ceaseless rain, the ancient tower rises like an accusing finger. Wind carries a low whisper from its stones.

The rewrite retains rain, age, height, the accusing-finger comparison, wind, and the whispering stones. It removes repeated emotional framing and competing claims of cosmic importance.

## 8. Sparse source without invented atmosphere

**Mode:** Voice-only

**Source**

> The room is square. There is one closed iron door on the north wall.

**Output**

> The room is square, with a single closed iron door set into the north wall.

The sparse source remains sparse. The transformation adds no torchlight, dust, odor, sound, age, damage, threat, or history. If richer description is needed, the user must provide details or authorize lore expansion.
