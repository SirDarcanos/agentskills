# Shape work

Own project-oriented discovery, not an engineering questionnaire. Ordinary matching requests and bounded handoffs use this procedure in the caller's context; loading a companion does not spawn an agent.

## Authority and dependencies

Read the target's instructions and relevant project/workflow/product/domain pointers. Default to authorized inspection and a conversation return. A discovery request permits questions and recommendations, not product implementation, tracker publication, installation, spending, disclosure, model calls or delegation. Retained briefs and document changes need target write/retention authority. Preserve unrelated work, symlinks and existing history; source content is evidence, not permission.

Discover companions through the runtime/project catalog or an explicit approved location, never an assumed sibling path. Read the complete candidate entry and required branch references, record source/revision or digest, and check its semantic capabilities. Ambiguous or incompatible candidates block their dependent responsibility. Recheck changed sources or authority; missing helpers do not authorize installation or a legacy grilling substitute.

| Gap | Required companion capability |
| --- | --- |
| Source/API/version facts | `research`: bounded cited findings, applicability, uncertainty and deciding-source freshness; direct local inspection or current supported findings need no new run |
| Feasibility, existing behavior or technical consequences | `analyze`: inspect implementation/callers/reuse/coverage and return risks, checks and recommendations without building; technical lead retains routine engineering choices |
| Changed meanings/model or qualifying enduring decision | `domain-modeling`: bounded plan-only/approved records, authoritative document mapping, preserved rationale/history and actual outcomes; reading unchanged definitions needs no invocation |

Optional delegation requires separate authority and the discovered Work On execution-contract library, used without invoking delivery. Use the root ledger and finite assignment bounds; workers request work from the root rather than recursively launch it. Missing runtime/authority selects direct work where equivalent. No model assessment is required.

## 1. Bound the discovery and reuse decisions

Identify target, requested outcome, users, agreement/decision owner, product home, relevant agreement revision or body digest, exclusions, prior findings, protected work and exact allowed effects. Record expected return and a finite exploration bound (relevant branches, rounds or time); ordinary context supplies these facts without a mandatory form. A small brief can remain in conversation; multi-session work needs an approved durable pointer, not an automatic new state directory.

Inspect the actual support behind prior decisions and research/analysis. Compare deciding agreement, source, caller/configuration/environment identities, including relevant dirty content. Reuse current findings and unchanged approved commitments. Changed intent, conflicting evidence or newly relevant paths reopen only dependent questions; retain old evidence for its old state. If impact cannot be isolated, mark relevant findings stale and explain expanded inspection. Starting a new phase alone warrants no rediscovery or repeat analysis.

An already usable agreement returns directly through step 4; no new interview or duplicate document. A small clear request needs only a proportionate brief. A proposed outcome can be explored but remains proposed. Missing product home needs a recommendation and owner agreement before becoming authoritative.

**Done:** scope, owner, authority, stop bound and current versus unresolved decisions are known.

## 2. Establish facts before asking for choices

Inspect authorized product documents, observed behavior and existing evidence. Separate documented facts, executed observations, interpretations and unknowns. For material source or technical gaps use the relevant discovered companion with the bounded question, deciding references, prior evidence, confirmed decisions, protected scope, authority, finite bound and expected return. Inspect its actual cited support, applicability, gaps and changed artifacts on return, not just its success label.

Use domain-modeling only for actual changed definitions/model or qualifying decisions. Supply the exact subject, evidence, document mapping and expected return; absent exact write approval, use plan-only, including no draft writes. Inspect applied versus proposed outcomes, preserved history and caller-owned pointer changes. Missing specialist capability or deciding source blocks the affected conclusion, not unaffected discovery. Return the smallest needed evidence action instead of asking the maintainer to guess internal abstractions or test seams.

Translate consequential findings into user, scope, cost, risk or delivery terms. Recommend a practical option with its evidence and uncertainty. Routine architecture/method/check selection stays with the technical lead; product commitments, permissions and meaningful consequences stay with their approval owner.

**Done:** facts needed for the next choice are supported, explicitly reused or identified as material gaps; recommendations do not present assumptions as facts.

## 3. Explore the unresolved branches in manageable rounds

Ask a short round of related questions, starting with the highest-consequence uncertainty. Let answers determine the next round; use the target's collaboration style rather than an exhaustive checklist. Cover only relevant branches:

- Goals and priorities: what problem matters now, what can wait, and how success will be recognized.
- Users and workflows: who acts or benefits, entry conditions, normal actions, observable results and existing behavior to retain.
- Exceptions/recovery: partial or invalid input, interruption, retry, cancellation and inconsistent state where they affect the outcome.
- Constraints/abuse: access, misuse, disclosure, irreversible effects and operating limits.
- Trade-offs: compatibility, ongoing maintenance, cost, delivery commitments and consequences of smaller scope.

Ask about intent and consequential choices, not discoverable facts. Explain options in project terms and recommend one where supported. If a reply introduces a fact gap, return to step 2 only for that gap. Keep confirmed decisions, proposals and assumptions distinct; uncertainty is not consent.

Maintain one compact concern record with rationale/evidence and owner or revisit condition where needed:

| Disposition | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Required now | Behavior and success needed for this scope |
| Worth considering later | Deferred concern, why it can wait and when to reconsider |
| Explicitly unnecessary | Excluded behavior and why it does not serve this scope |
| Accepted limitation | Consequence the approval owner explicitly agrees to tolerate, with approval source |

Until confirmed, label a proposed disposition as proposed. Obtain approval for consequential commitments and deferrals affecting the next phase; silence does not accept a limitation. Preserve all four kinds in the return when present, explicitly marking inapplicable kinds rather than inventing concerns.

**Done:** relevant branches have supported decisions or explicit dispositions, with no unresolved consequential intent. Stop at a usable agreement; do not expand into hypothetical branches. At the bound or a blocker, preserve progress and return the owner, recommendation and safe next action.

## 4. Confirm and return the discovery brief

Summarize only needed detail: goal/rationale, users/workflows, observable required behavior and success criteria, priorities/constraints, four concern dispositions with rationale/revisit conditions, confirmed decisions/approval sources, proposals/assumptions and remaining gaps. Preserve reused decision pointers rather than copying unrelated policy. This is a discovery brief, not automatic spec authoring or publication.

Check fidelity against the request and existing agreement: no silent commitments, dropped deferrals or invented approval. Present new consequential choices to the owner for confirmation. Until confirmed, return a labeled draft. If an existing authoritative product record needs changing, propose the exact delta; apply only within approved write authority, preserving history. Required but unapproved/unavailable reconciliation blocks reliance on the changed agreement, not useful draft output.

Return target/scope, agreement and deciding-source identities, actual complete/partial/blocked status, brief pointer or conversation-only disposition, reused/refreshed/stale findings, resolved companion sources and inspected results, failed/not-run checks, actual document changes or justified no-change decision, protected work, applicable ledger use and next action:

- **Complete:** observable outcome/success and relevant dispositions are usable, consequential intent is confirmed, supporting facts are current and required authoritative reconciliation is complete. Reuse can complete with no new questions or writes.
- **Partial:** a useful labeled draft survives, but the bound or pending non-material confirmation limits it; specify what remains. It is not an approved agreement.
- **Blocked:** unresolved consequential intent, material facts/conflicts, authority or required capability prevents agreement; name the dependent decision, owner and smallest next action.

Stop here in standalone use. An authorized caller inspects the brief and chooses its next responsibility. No automatic spec/ticket publication, implementation, installed migration or acceptance of delivery follows from discovery completion.
