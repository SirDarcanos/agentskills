# Review input and caller contract

Read on standalone entry and bounded handoff receipt. Ordinary requests supply these facts through conversation and inspected context; no mandatory form, parent spec or delivery invocation is required.

## 1. Normalize the request

Record:

- Target, bounded purpose, authoritative approved agreement revision/body digest, acceptance IDs or checkable brief outcomes, exclusions and confirmed decisions.
- Deciding sources and actual committed/dirty work, raw checks with command/environment/baseline and checked-input identities, relevant standards/policy pointers, uncertainty and missing inputs.
- Inspection authority and approval source; separate reviewer delegation, disclosure, retention and safe-check permissions. Loading this package grants none of them.
- Owned files/behavior/interfaces, protected work, branch/base/integration and dependency evidence where applicable, prior checkpoint/finding lineage and root execution-record pointer.
- Expected axis reports, approved result location, completion/blocking conditions and, when delegated, reviewer identity, isolation, finite bounds and reserved worker slot.

Inspect the primary agreement, sources and raw evidence. A lead's preferred verdict, completed checklist, ticket closure or child pass does not establish coverage. Missing approved behavior blocks Spec coverage; retain any useful Standards-only inspection as partial, not complete acceptance review. Ask only for material unresolved intent or authority, not an artificial spec hierarchy.

**Complete when:** each material input is inspected and current, explicitly inapplicable, or an affected blocker.

## 2. Resolve the execution dependency

Before delegated review, discover compatible `work-on` through the runtime/project catalog or an approved explicit location. Read its complete entry and execution/handoff contract as a **read-only library**, without invoking its delivery gate. Record resolved source/revision or digest. It must preserve shared capacity, root launch ownership, finite lifecycle, stopped/isolated replacement and checkpoint/finding lineage across callers and resume. Ambiguous, missing or incompatible candidates block delegated review; never install or use a legacy review command as fallback. Recheck changed source, authority or assignment.

A standalone caller is the bounded root for its authorized review. A `work-on` single-change lead or `work-on-spec` overall lead retains its existing execution record; ticket workers request reviewer assignments from that root rather than launch another pool. Use the library's limits and available slots, not a new per-skill allowance. Local single-change review and whole-spec review use the same procedure with different agreements and snapshots; child reports cannot certify an integrated snapshot.

No delivery companion is needed for plain direct inspection. Without independent contexts, return the independent-review blocker and label direct inspection according to its actual relationship to the authors. Classifier output or repeated model agreement cannot satisfy reviewer independence; no optional model is required or automatically invoked.

**Complete when:** the authorized root has resolved the execution contract and reserved bounded independent contexts, or the dependent review route is blocked.

## 3. Reuse and invalidate evidence

Compare deciding input identities and source coverage on receipt/resume. Consume current complete analysis/investigation/checks without calling their owners again merely because review starts. Fill only material gaps. Changed code, callers, agreement, policy, environment or newly relevant paths invalidate dependent evidence; unclear impact invalidates all relevant evidence. Apply the snapshot owner's [scope contract](scope.md), not a caller's HEAD-only inventory.

Keep prior findings/dispositions and spent checkpoint history outside neutral reviewer inputs as required by the [packet contract](report.md#independent-assignments). Preserve original axis reports unchanged; the caller owns the disposition record. For a repair, inspect the actual changed artifacts and refreshed raw checks. An old passing log is stale if its deciding inputs changed, even if its own bytes still match. Significant repairs require renewed affected independent review; retaining evidence for a narrowly non-significant change requires explicit impact analysis and recorded scope/delta equivalence, with state-sensitive checks rerun. Neither a new specialist nor resume resets lineage or budgets.

**Complete when:** current reusable evidence and stale/gap sets are explicit, with the smallest affected check/review renewal and its owner identified. Stop at the review return; repairs, acceptance, PR action, merge, closure and release remain caller gates.
